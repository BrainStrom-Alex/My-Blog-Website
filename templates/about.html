About Me – Ravish Kumar Verma

I am Ravish Kumar Verma, an Electrical Engineering undergraduate at the National Institute of Technology, Hamirpur. I am passionate about technology, innovation, robotics, embedded systems, and electric vehicles. My primary interest lies in understanding how hardware and software work together to create intelligent machines and automated systems. I enjoy learning through practical projects, experimenting with electronic components, programming microcontrollers, and exploring new engineering technologies. My long-term vision is to contribute to the development of advanced robotics, autonomous systems, and next-generation electric vehicles.

About BrainStorm

BrainStorm is my vision of building an innovative technology company focused on robotics, automation, embedded systems, and electric vehicle technologies. The goal of BrainStorm is to transform creative ideas into practical engineering solutions. We aim to explore intelligent machines, autonomous technologies, smart hardware, and innovative products that can solve real-world problems. I believe that great innovations begin with curiosity, experimentation, and the courage to think differently. BrainStorm represents this mindset: Think Beyond Limits. Engineer the Future. My ultimate ambition is to build BrainStorm into a technology-driven company that develops impactful products and contributes to the future of robotics and intelligent mobility.
